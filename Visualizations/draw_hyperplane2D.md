# draw_hyperplane2D(...)
Draws a hyperplane on a 2D plot.

## Parameters
- `hyperplane : Hyperplane`: an `mplusa` hyperplane.
- `color : str`: color to render the hyperplane with.
- `scale : float`: the scale of the extent of the visualization.
- `show : bool`: whether or not to invoke `matplotlib.pyplot.show` at the end.
